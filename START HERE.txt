####################################################################
#  STEP 1 - CHANNEL   (2 minutes, and it matters more than it looks)
####################################################################

Click "Channel". Fill it in honestly.

This is not decoration. It is what ClipForge uses to decide WHICH
moments become clips. Right now it knows nothing about you, so it picks
whatever seems generally interesting - which is exactly how you get
clips that are fine but aren't yours.

The two boxes that do the work:

  "What is this channel about?"
      Be specific. Not "interviews" but "long-form fighter interviews
      cut down to the moments where they say something they regret."
      The vaguer this is, the more generic your clips will be.

  "Never clip anything about"
      A hard block. Use it.

Your handle goes on every clip and in every caption.

You can change this any time. New clips follow the new answer; clips
already found don't re-shuffle.


####################################################################
#  STEP 2 - ACCOUNTS
####################################################################

Click "Accounts". Each platform shows a dot:

    grey   nothing added yet
    amber  details saved - needs your one Connect click
    green  done, it can post

Nothing you type here is ever shown back on screen, written to the
project's saved history, or sent anywhere except the platform it
belongs to.


  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  YOUTUBE - do this one first, it is the only one with no waiting
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  You already enabled the API. That was half of it. The other half:

    1. console.cloud.google.com
    2. APIs & Services  ->  Credentials
    3. + CREATE CREDENTIALS  ->  OAuth client ID
    4. Application type:  Desktop app  ->  Create
    5. Click the download icon. You get a .json file.
    6. Open it in Notepad, select all, copy.
    7. Paste it into the YouTube box on the Accounts page. Save.
    8. Click "Connect YouTube". Approve the Google screen.

  Green dot. YouTube can post.

  Worth knowing: YouTube's free quota is about 6 uploads a day.
  ClipForge already knows this and holds the rest for tomorrow rather
  than failing. You don't have to do anything about it.


  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  TIKTOK
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  Make sure you are on developers.tiktok.com - the DEVELOPER site.
  Not the normal TikTok app. If last night you only made a regular
  TikTok account, that account is still needed (it's what gets posted
  to), but the developer signup is the part that matters here.

    1. Manage apps -> create an app
    2. Add the product:   Content Posting API
    3. Request the scope: video.publish
    4. Redirect URI - paste exactly, no trailing slash:
           http://127.0.0.1:8000/oauth/tiktok/callback
    5. Copy the Client key and Client secret into the TikTok box. Save.
    6. Click "Connect TikTok".

  ** READ THIS - it is the one thing that might change your plans **

  Until TikTok formally reviews your app, it can ONLY post private
  videos that nobody but you can see, 5 a day. That is TikTok's rule
  and there is no way around it. Public posting requires their audit:
  2 to 4 weeks, usually several rounds of back and forth.

  So TikTok will genuinely work tomorrow - privately. Which is a good
  reason to start the audit early rather than last.

  ** IF THE REDIRECT URI BOX REFUSES THAT ADDRESS **
  Some portals demand https and reject 127.0.0.1. If that happens,
  stop and tell me the exact wording of the error. Don't improvise a
  different address - a wrong redirect URI doesn't fail now, it fails
  later in a way that's genuinely confusing to unpick. I have a route
  around it; I just need to know which wall you hit.


  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTAGRAM - the long one. Leave it until the others are green.
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  Before any developer steps, the account itself has to be right:

    - It must be a PROFESSIONAL / BUSINESS account.
      NOT Creator. The posting API refuses Creator accounts for Reels.
      (Instagram app -> Settings -> Account type -> switch to Business)
    - It must be linked to a Facebook Page.

  Then:

    1. developers.facebook.com -> My Apps -> Create App
    2. Link it to that Facebook Page / Business account
    3. Copy the App ID and App secret into the Instagram box
    4. You also need your Instagram user ID (the numeric one)
    5. Save, then Connect

  Meta reviews the two permissions this needs and wants a screen
  recording of the whole flow. 2 to 4 weeks, same as TikTok.


  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  CLIP STORAGE - Instagram only. Ignore until Instagram is approved.
  ----------------------------------------------------------------
  Instagram refuses to accept a video file. It insists on fetching the
  clip from a public web address. Your clips live on this PC, so each
  clip has to sit somewhere public for the ~60 seconds Instagram needs
  to grab it, and then be deleted.

  I built that. It's done and tested. It needs an S3-compatible
  bucket - Cloudflare R2 is the cheap one, roughly a few dollars a
  month.

  I have NOT signed up for anything. That is your card and your call,
  so it waits for you.

  Until it's set up, Instagram posts simply wait in the queue.
  Nothing else is affected. YouTube and TikTok don't need this at all.
